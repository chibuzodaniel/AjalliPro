Customer incentive tiers
Bonus bags awarded based on bags bought in a single sale.
Min bagsMax bagsBonus bags
Special weekly incentive: customers reaching 500 bags in a week get +5 bonus bags. Drivers reaching 1,000 bags in a week get +10 bonus bags. These are fixed thresholds per the company policy.
Production requirement (today)
Suggested bags to produce today to cover expected demand and keep a safety buffer.
About this system
This is a working prototype: sign-in is a simple role picker (no password/verification), and data is stored per-browser-session via the app's shared storage — it is not a hardened, production-secure multi-tenant backend. Weekly customer emails are composed in-app but not actually delivered; wiring real delivery, CBN-grade auth, and audit logging would need a proper backend (e.g. a small Node/Postgres service) behind this interface.
